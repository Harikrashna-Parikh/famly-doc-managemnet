## 1. Getting Started — Registration

Before you can use the app, your family's **Admin** must have already set up the locker. Once the app is live, follow these steps to create your account:

### Step-by-step:

1. Open the Family Document Locker URL shared by your Admin (e.g. `https://your-family.github.io/family-documents/`).
2. You will see the **Login** screen.
3. Tap the **Register** tab at the top of the form.
4. Fill in the registration form:

   | Field         | What to enter                                      |
   |---------------|----------------------------------------------------|
   | **Full Name** | Your real name (e.g. "Priya Parikh")               |
   | **Email**     | A valid email address you have access to            |
   | **Password**  | A strong password (minimum 6 characters)            |

5. Tap **Register Account**.
6. You will be logged in automatically with the **👤 Member** role.

> **Note:** The first person to register is automatically assigned the **👑 Admin** role. All subsequent registrations receive the **👤 Member** role. If you need Admin access, ask your existing Admin to promote you from the Settings page.

---

## 2. Logging In

If you already have an account:

1. Open the app URL.
2. Make sure the **Login** tab is selected.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Tap **Login**.
5. You will be redirected to your **Dashboard**.

> 💡 **Tip:** You can toggle password visibility using the 👁️ eye icon next to the password field.

---

## 3. Understanding the Dashboard

After logging in, you will see the **Dashboard** — your home screen.

### What you'll see:

- **Welcome message** with your name at the top.
- **Family Member Folder Cards** — These are the document folders you have been granted access to by the Admin.

### What each folder card shows:

| Element          | Description                            |
|------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Name**         | The family member's name or label      |
| **Document Count** | Number of documents in that folder   |
| **Open Folder**  | Button to browse that member's documents |

### Important:

- You will **only** see folders that the Admin has specifically granted you access to.
- If you see **"No authorized folders yet. Contact Admin to get access."**, it means the Admin hasn't assigned any folders to your account yet. Reach out to your family Admin.

## 4. Viewing Documents

Once you open a family member's folder, you can browse and view their documents.

### Navigating Documents:

1. Tap **Open Folder** on any family member card.
2. You will see all documents organized by **category** (Identity, Education, Vehicle, Insurance, Property, Financial, Other).
3. Use the **horizontal category tabs** at the top to filter by category (e.g. tap "🪪 Identity" to see only identity documents).
4. Use the **search bar** to find a specific document by name or category.

### Viewing a Document:

1. Tap on any **document card** (or tap the 👁️ eye icon).
2. The **Document Viewer** modal will open, showing:
   - **Document Title** and metadata (category, file size, date).
   - **Preview** — Images are shown inline; PDFs are rendered in an embedded viewer.
3. From the viewer, you can:

   | Button          | Action                                         |
   |-----------------|-------------------------------------------------|
   | **Open in Tab** | Opens the document in a new browser tab          |
   | **Print**       | Prints the document directly                     |
   | **Share**       | Shares the document via WhatsApp, Email, or link |
   | **Download**    | Opens the custom download options modal          |

4. Tap **❌** or the dark backdrop area to close the viewer.

## 5. Fullscreen Photo Viewer

For image documents (photos of Aadhaar, PAN cards, marksheets, etc.), you can view them in a **distraction-free fullscreen** mode:

1. Open any image document in the Document Viewer (as described above).
2. **Tap/click on the photo preview** — notice the zoom-in cursor (🔍).
3. The image will open in a **dark fullscreen overlay**, filling your entire screen.
4. To exit fullscreen, either:
   - Tap the **❌** close button (top-right corner), or
   - Tap anywhere on the dark background area, or
   - Press the **Escape** key on your keyboard.

> 💡 This is especially useful on mobile to inspect small text on documents like Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, or marksheet grades.

## 6. Downloading Documents

You can download any document you have access to, with advanced customization options.

### Quick Download (Original File):

1. Tap the **⬇️ Download** icon on any document card in the list, or
2. Open the document viewer and tap **Download**.
3. The **Download Options** modal will appear.
4. Leave all settings as **"Original"** and tap **Download**.
5. Your browser will download the original file as-is.

### Custom Download (Convert, Resize, or Compress):

The download modal offers powerful options:

| Option               | What it does                                                        |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Format**           | Convert between Original, JPEG (.jpg), PNG (.png), or PDF (.pdf)    |
| **Dimensions**       | Keep Original, or set Custom width × height (with aspect ratio lock 🔒) |
| **File Size**        | Keep Original, or set a Custom target range (Min KB – Max KB)       |

**Example Use Cases:**

- **Passport-size photo:** Set dimensions to 600×800 and format to JPEG.
- **Reduced file for email:** Set max size to 100 KB to compress the image.
- **PDF conversion:** Convert an image document to a PDF for official submissions.

After configuring, tap **Download** — the app processes everything in your browser (no server upload needed) and saves the file.

## 7. Printing Documents

You can print any document directly from the app:

1. Open the document in the **Document Viewer**.
2. Tap the **🖨️ Print** button.
3. For **images**: A clean print layout will open containing only the document image — no headers, titles, or app UI elements.
4. For **PDFs**: The document opens in a new tab and the browser's print dialog is triggered automatically.
5. Select your printer and print.

> 💡 The print output is intentionally clean — only the document content is printed, making it suitable for official submissions.

---

## 8. Sharing Documents

You can share any document with others via multiple methods.

### On Mobile (Native Share):

1. Tap the **🔗 Share** icon on any document card, or tap **Share** inside the viewer.
2. Your phone's **native share sheet** will appear (the same one used by WhatsApp, Photos, etc.).
3. The **actual document file** is attached directly — not just a link.
4. Choose any app (WhatsApp, Gmail, Telegram, etc.) to share.

### On Desktop (Share Modal):

If native sharing isn't available, a **Share Modal** will open with these options:

| Option           | Action                                                         |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **📎 Share File** | Attaches and shares the original document file                |
| **💬 WhatsApp**   | Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the link  |
| **📧 Email**      | Opens your email client with subject and secure link           |
| **📋 Copy Link**  | Copies a temporary secure link to your clipboard               |

> **Note:** Shared links are **temporary** — they expire after **24 hours** for security. The recipient must open the link within that window.

## 9. Dark Mode / Light Mode

The app supports both light and dark themes:

1. Look for the **🌙 / ☀️** toggle button in the top-right corner of the header.
2. Tap it to switch between **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**.
3. Your preference is saved automatically and will persist across sessions.

> The app also respects your device's system theme setting by default.

## 10. Forgot Password

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Open the app and go to the **Login** tab.
2. Enter your **email address** in the email field.
3. Tap the **Forgot Password?** link below the password field.
4. Check your email inbox for a **password reset link** from Supabase.
5. Click the link and set a new password.
6. Return to the app and log in with your new password.

---

## 11. Logging Out

1. Tap the **Logout** button in the top-right corner of the header (next to your name and role badge).
2. You will be immediately signed out and returned to the Login screen.
3. Your session is cleared — no one can access your account without logging in again.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ I registered but I can't see any document folders.
Your Admin needs to **grant you access** to specific family member folders. Contact your Admin and ask them to add your email under **Settings → User Access Management**.

### ❓ Can I upload or delete documents?
No. Only **Admins** can upload new documents or delete existing ones. Members have **read-only** access (view, download, print, share).

### ❓ How secure are my documents?
Very secure:
- All documents are stored in a **private storage bucket** — there are no permanent public URLs.
- Every time you view or download a document, a **temporary signed URL** is generated that expires in **5 minutes**.
- Shared links expire in **24 hours**.
- Row Level Security (RLS) ensures you can only access folders assigned to you.

### ❓ Can I access the app offline?
No. The app requires an active internet connection to communicate with Supabase for authentication and document access. If you go offline, an **orange warning banner** will appear at the top of the screen.

### ❓ What file types can I view?
| Type       | Supported Formats                    |
|------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Images** | `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`     |
| **PDFs**   | `.pdf`                               |

### ❓ Can I be promoted to Admin?
Yes, but only by an existing Admin. Ask your family Admin to go to **Settings → User Access Management** and click **"Make Admin"** next to your name.

### ❓ What happens if my account is deactivated?
If an Admin deactivates your account, you will see the message **"Your account has been deactivated. Contact Admin."** and be automatically logged out. Contact your Admin to reactivate your account.
